Stress-testing a metals hedge: what the numbers actually tell you

Completed · June 2026
Problem→Thinking→Architecture→Impact

Built from methodology I encountered on a real desk evaluation: how do you stress-test a metals derivatives book when the risk isn't one exposure but two — front-end slippage risk and back-end holding risk? The project covers liquidity screening via open interest, time spread decomposition, rolling N-day stress statistics (max, p99, p95, min), and the DV01 angle specific to precious metals — where long gold means being short lease rates and short interest rate sensitivity.

Hedging a position: what six strategies actually cost you

Completed · May 2026
Problem→Thinking→Architecture→Impact

A payoff visualiser for collars, protective puts, call spreads, bear put spreads, and straddles — applied to a 100-unit position at INR 2,800. The zero-cost collar costs no premium but caps your upside at INR 20,400. The protective put floors your loss but shifts your breakeven to INR 2,848. The straddle needs a 6.75% move in either direction before you see a rupee of profit. A live tool lets you drag the price yourself and watch every strategy's payoff update.

P&L Attribution Tool

Planned · August 2026

Replicates the P&L attribution methodology used in trading risk management: decomposing daily P&L into Delta P&L, Gamma P&L, Vega P&L, Theta decay, and unexplained residual. This is exactly how derivatives desks report performance — breaking down what drove the number so traders and risk managers can understand it. Built in Python with a clean tabular output format.
